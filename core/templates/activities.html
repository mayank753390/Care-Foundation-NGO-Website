Our Activities

At Mayank Care Foundation, we focus on diverse programs that address key challenges faced by marginalized communities.

  • Educational Support

    Providing scholarships, school supplies, and after-school tutoring to underprivileged children to ensure access to quality education.

  • Healthcare Camps

    Organizing free health check-ups, awareness sessions, and vaccination drives to improve community health and well-being.

  • Women Empowerment

    Conducting skill development workshops, self-help groups, and micro-finance training to enable women’s economic independence.

  • Environmental Conservation

    Leading tree plantation drives, clean-up campaigns, and awareness programs for sustainable environmental practices.

  • Disaster Relief

    Providing immediate assistance and rehabilitation support to communities affected by natural disasters.

Each activity is designed to create lasting change by addressing the root causes of social and economic challenges. Join us and be part of this transformative journey.
